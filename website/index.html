questions

what do I get?

think of it as the agent you never had, minus the hidden cut. real fees and budgets, shared openly. answers on usage, contracts and invoices. templates you can just use. and creatives to find, ask and work with. one place for everything freelance. you stay your own agent, we’ve got your back.

do I have to share anonymously?

it’s up to you. share anonymously (no name, no email, nothing that points back to you) or put your name on it. fee numbers only ever show as ranges, once at least five creatives have shared, never per person. and what you charge is always your call.

can I just take a peek first?

of course. joining is free and comes with no strings. sign up, look around, bring a friend, leave whenever you like.

who’s behind fee fee.?

it was started by Femke Juffermans, a dutch creative director and photographer. but fee fee. belongs to everyone who joins: freelance creatives everywhere, figuring it out together.
