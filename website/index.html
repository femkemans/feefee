questions

what is fee fee.?

a community, for freelance creatives worldwide. freelance creatives often work alone, each on their own island. fee fee. connects us: share real numbers, ask each other questions, find the people you work with and help the next creative say the right number. open to every freelance creative, everywhere.

is this for me?

yes, if you freelance in a creative job. any level, anywhere: photographers, stylists, MUAs, assistants, models, producers and everyone else on set. just starting out? you’re exactly who this is for.

do I have to pay?

no. joining costs nothing and you don’t pay anything now. just sign up, no stress.

is sharing my fee anonymous?

yes. no name, no email. fees only show grouped, never per person.

what happens after I sign up?

you’re on the list. when the doors open, the first fee drop lands in your inbox. no spam, just a few letters. unsubscribe anytime.

can I bring my people?

please do. invite the creatives you work with through your own link. the more of us there are, the better the numbers get for everyone, and the earlier you’re in.

who’s behind fee fee.?

it was started by Femke Juffermans, a Dutch creative director and photographer. but fee fee. belongs to everyone who joins and shares: freelance creatives everywhere, helping each other, not just one circle.
