house rules.

fee fee. only works if people can trust what they read here. so a few rules, for questions, answers, jobs and the index.

1. real names

Everyone joins with their own name. No fake accounts, no accounts for someone else. Your fees stay private: numbers only show as ranges, never next to your name unless you choose it.

2. be kind, be honest

Answer the way you'd want to be answered when you were starting out. Disagree with the idea, not the person. Share real numbers and real experience, not guesses dressed up as facts.

3. no naming and shaming

Don't name clients, agencies or other creatives to complain about them. Talk about what happened and what helped. Bad experiences are useful; public blame is not.

4. jobs are real jobs

A job post is a real job with a real date. Always say the fee, or write "open to quotes". No unpaid work dressed up as "exposure", no "test shoots" that are really campaigns, no recruiting for courses or schemes.

5. no selling, no spam

No ads, affiliate links or sales pitches in questions and answers. Your own work belongs on your link page and in the index.

6. fees, not price agreements

Share what jobs paid. Never agree with others on what everyone should charge: that's against competition law, and it isn't what fee fee. is for. What you charge is always your own call.

7. keep it private

What someone shares in a reply to your job, or on a call, stays between you. Don't post screenshots of other people's messages.

when something's wrong

Every answer and every job has a small report link. femke reads every report. Posts that break these rules are hidden, and people who keep breaking them lose their account. Questions? hello@feefee.co.
